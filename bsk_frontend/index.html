    How to Use

    1. Copy the raw text from any suspicious email or message you have received.
    2. Sanitize the text by double-checking that no personal information (passwords, bank details) remains inside it, just in case.
    3. Paste the contents into the text field above in our analysis tool.
    4. Submit and wait a few seconds. The AI will cross-reference language patterns and flag potential phishing markers.
